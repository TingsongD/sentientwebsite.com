## Basis to Validate

This determination should be validated against the final production facts, including:

- whether Robanka Inc. has an establishment in the EU or UK
- whether SentientWeb offers goods or services to EU/UK individuals
- whether SentientWeb monitors EU/UK individuals' behavior

- whether any EU member-state or UK-specific rule imposes a local representative or DPO duty

## Current Product Controls

- The public website and assistant are not directed to children under 18.
- The assistant does not intentionally collect sensitive personal data.
- The assistant is disclosed as automated and not a human.
- The assistant is loaded only after consent and 18+ confirmation.
- Privacy requests are available through `/data-request`.
- Persistent assistant-related retention is attributable to Google’s Gemini / Gemini Live services; CRM retention is attributable to HubSpot.
