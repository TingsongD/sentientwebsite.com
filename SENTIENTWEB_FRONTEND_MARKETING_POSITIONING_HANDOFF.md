# SentientWeb Marketing Positioning Handoff

Current positioning:

Revenue recovery orchestration for modern subscription businesses.

SentientWeb finds revenue leaks across your website, billing, CRM, scheduler, and messaging stack, then calls the right tool to recover the moment.

SentientWeb should be described as the orchestration layer above the customer’s existing technology stack. It uses the customer’s existing website, CRM, billing, scheduler, messaging, routing, support, and webhook tools as execution surfaces to accomplish business outcomes.

Avoid framing SentientWeb as limited to a single vertical, single tool, single scheduler, or single recovery use case. Demo recovery can appear as one use case, but the company category is revenue recovery orchestration.

Preferred terms:

- Revenue recovery orchestration
- Subscription businesses
- Existing tech stack
- Stack-ready context
- Qualified recovered actions
- Business outcomes
- Calls the right tool
- Orchestration layer above your stack
