## New Insights from the 20-CEO Cohort

### 1. "Public proof rights optional" is universally praised
Not a single CEO in the 20-person cohort objected to the case-study requirement. This friction point from Rounds 1-2 is **fully resolved.**

### 2. The "replacement vs. augmentation" narrative still needs work
5 CEOs (ChargeFast, DataVault, CloudPeak, PropSync, and echoing ClaraLegal from R3) still want to know what SentientWeb **replaces**, not just what it orchestrates. ChargeFast specifically: *"I'm not buying another unvetted AI tool — I'm replacing Drift."* A clearer "this replaces generic chatbots, not your routing/scheduling" message would help.

### 3. Vertical solution pages create mixed signals
- **InsureFlow:** The /solutions/insurance page targets instant-quote recovery (B2C), but InsureFlow sells B2B SaaS underwriting automation. Mismatch.
- **ComplyAI:** The /solutions/financial-services page targets mortgage lenders, not B2B fintech compliance. Mismatch.
- **LearnPro:** The /solutions/edtech page targets student enrollment, not B2B corporate training. Mismatch.
- **ShipTrack:** No logistics-specific page exists. Generic B2B SaaS path only.

**Pattern:** Vertical pages are built for B2C use cases within those industries, not B2B SaaS companies selling TO those industries. This is confusing buyers.

### 4. The "Instant Demo Preview" is being discovered
CodeSignal and PropSync CEOs both tried to use the "Instant Demo Preview" feature but found it requires a sales call, not truly instant. This created mild friction — expectations weren't met.

### 5. Calendly vs. Chili Piper tension persists
3 Salesforce CEOs (RevenueForge, ChargeFast, DataVault) flagged that their stack uses Chili Piper, not Calendly. SentientWeb's Calendly-native booking creates a compatibility question. The FAQ says "Does this replace Chili Piper?" but the answer needs to be more prominent.

### 6. HubSpot free tier compatibility is a real question
4 CEOs (TeamBridge, BrandPulse, Recruitly, LearnLoop from R3) are on HubSpot free or starter tiers. None could find explicit confirmation that the integration works with free tiers. This is a hidden blocker for the sub-$3M segment.

## Recommendations — Priority Order

### P0: Get SOC 2 Type II Certified
**Impact:** Blocking 17/20 CEOs to varying degrees. For 5 CEOs (DataVault, SecureDocs, InsureFlow, ComplyAI, ChargeFast), it's a hard pre-pilot gate. For the other 12, it's a procurement friction point that delays deals.
- **Estimated revenue at risk:** DataVault alone = $65K ACV × 4 deals/year = $260K pipeline. Combined 5 hard-blocked CEOs = $600K+ pipeline.

### P0: Clarify "We Replace Generic Chatbots"
**Impact:** 5 CEOs want a replacement narrative. Create a page or FAQ section: "What does SentientWeb replace?" Answer: "Generic chat widgets, static 'Book a Demo' forms, and unqualified inbound that wastes AE time. We orchestrate your scheduling and routing tools, we don't replace them."

### P1: Add 1 Quantified Case Study
**Impact:** 12/20 CEOs cited lack of social proof. Even one case study with a named customer (with permission) showing "X demos recovered = $Y pipeline added" would eliminate the "design partner anxiety."

### P1: Fix Vertical Solution Pages
**Impact:** 4 CEOs found industry pages mismatched (B2C vs. B2B). Either rename pages to "B2B SaaS for [Industry]" or create separate tracks for (a) SaaS companies selling TO the industry and (b) companies IN the industry doing demo recovery.

### P2: Confirm HubSpot Free Tier Compatibility
**Impact:** 4 CEOs on free/starter tiers couldn't find confirmation. Add a line to the HubSpot integration page: "Works with HubSpot Free, Starter, Professional, and Enterprise tiers."

### P2: Clarify Chili Piper Positioning
**Impact:** 3 Salesforce CEOs use Chili Piper, not Calendly. Make the Chili Piper answer more prominent: "We book through Calendly in v1. If you use Chili Piper, we can route qualified visitors to your Chili Piper booking links via custom integration — let's discuss during your fit review."

### P2: Make "Instant Demo Preview" Actually Instant
**Impact:** 2 CEOs tried it and were disappointed. Either make it truly self-serve or reframe as "Request a Preview."
