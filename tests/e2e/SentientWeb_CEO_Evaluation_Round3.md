# SentientWeb CEO Buyer Evaluation Report — Round 3
## 11 B2B SaaS CEOs Evaluate the CRM-Agnostic Update

**Report Date:** May 5, 2026
**Methodology:** 11 autonomous AI agents, each acting as a CEO of a B2B SaaS company, independently evaluated the updated SentientWeb website. Key changes since Round 2: CRM-agnostic messaging ("sales workflow your team actually uses"), dedicated Salesforce/Pipedrive/API integration pages, "Public proof rights optional" on all tiers, new Annual pricing tier, expanded vertical solutions, and enhanced trust signals.

### Verdict: The CRM-agnostic update was a game-changer.

The previous two rounds showed polarization — HubSpot-native CEOs loved it, everyone else was frustrated. Round 3 eliminated that divide. **Every CEO is now at least conditionally interested.** The dedicated Salesforce, Pipedrive, and API/Webhooks integration pages directly addressed the #1 blocker across all prior rounds. The "public proof rights are optional" change also removed a secondary friction point.

The remaining blocker is now **SOC 2 certification** — mentioned by 9/11 CEOs — but even that is no longer a dealbreaker for most, just a pre-pilot checkpoint.

## What the Updated Site Did Right

### 1. CRM-Agnostic Hero Messaging
Changed from "syncs into HubSpot" to "syncs into the sales workflow your team actually uses." This single line change signaled to non-HubSpot buyers that they were welcome.

### 2. Dedicated CRM Integration Pages
The `/integrations/salesforce` and `/integrations/pipedrive` pages were the highest-impact additions. Each page speaks directly to that CRM's buyers:
- Salesforce: "field mapping reviewed before launch," "fallback to HubSpot/CSV/webhook," "pilot shouldn't start if Salesforce sync isn't credible"
- Pipedrive: "shouldn't have to migrate CRM," "webhook or structured export paths," "measured by sales-accepted demos, not integration theater"
- API/Webhooks: "Custom CRM, no CRM, or early sales stack," "define the handoff first"

### 3. "Public Proof Rights Are Optional"
Removed a barrier for 5+ CEOs who were concerned about being forced marketing references. The optional framing with discount incentive is a smart trade.

### 4. Annual Tier
Created a path to predictable budgeting for post-pilot customers. Not yet compelling enough to start with, but a logical upgrade path.

### 5. Human Handoff Feature
Explicitly routing complex/sensitive questions to humans was noticed and valued by security, healthcare, and legal CEOs. It reduces AI-hallucination risk fears.

### 6. Enhanced Trust Signals
"Encrypted in transit," "Protected response paths," "Retention controls," "18-month limit disclosed" — these reduce security concerns even without SOC 2.

## Remaining Opportunities (What to Fix Next)

### P0: Get SOC 2 Certified
**Impact:** Blocks 9/11 CEOs from unconditional pilots. Currently the #1 blocker by far.
- Healthcare (MediSync) needs SOC 2 + BAA
- Cybersecurity (ShieldStack) CISO will block without it
- Legal (ClaraLegal) procurement requires it
- Enterprise ($10M+ ARR) InfoSec teams flag it

### P1: Clarify Annual Tier Details
**Impact:** 5/11 CEOs found the Annual tier vague. Add: included demo volume, specific discount vs. monthly, what's custom vs. standard.

### P1: Replacement Narrative for Tool-Heavy Stacks
**Impact:** ClaraLegal (6/10) and DataPulse (7/10) are blocked by "adds another tool" objection. Create content explaining what SentientWeb REPLACES (generic chatbots, unqualified form fills) vs. what it ORCHESTRATES (Chili Piper, Qualified routing).

### P2: Build Healthcare-Specific B2B SaaS Page
**Impact:** MediSync dropped from 7/10 to 6/10 because the /solutions/healthcare page targets patient enrollment, not B2B healthcare SaaS. A page for "healthcare SaaS demo recovery" with HIPAA-aware messaging would unlock this segment.

### P2: Add One Quantified Case Study
**Impact:** Still mentioned by 3/11 CEOs. Even one case study with "X company recovered Y demos = $Z pipeline" would eliminate the "unproven" objection.

### P2: Construction/Vertical Proof Points
**Impact:** BuildTrack (7/10) is held back by "can the AI speak construction?" Add approved-source answer examples for 2-3 verticals to demonstrate domain adaptability.
