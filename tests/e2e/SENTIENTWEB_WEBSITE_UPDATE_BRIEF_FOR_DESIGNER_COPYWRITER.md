# SentientWeb Website Copy Brief

SentientWeb should read as a complete revenue recovery orchestration product for modern subscription businesses.

## Company Positioning

Revenue recovery orchestration for modern subscription businesses.

SentientWeb finds revenue leaks across your website, billing, CRM, scheduler, and messaging stack, then calls the right tool to recover the moment.

## Product Explanation

SentientWeb is the orchestration layer above the customer’s existing stack. It does not replace the stack. It uses the customer’s existing website, CRM, billing, scheduler, messaging, routing, support, and webhook tools as execution surfaces to accomplish business outcomes.

The website should not imply that SentientWeb is limited to one vertical, one CRM, one scheduler, or one use case. Demo recovery can appear as a use case, but the company category is broader revenue recovery orchestration.

## Preferred Language

- Revenue recovery orchestration
- Subscription businesses
- Existing tech stack
- Stack-ready context
- Qualified recovered actions
- Business outcomes
- Calls the right tool
- Orchestration layer above your stack

## Avoid

- B2B-only positioning
- Tool-required positioning
- Narrow launch-positioning phrasing
- “Wedge” language
- Demo-only product framing
- “Public,” “private,” or “fictitious” as customer-facing labels

## Use Case Framing

Use cases should read like real product workflows:

- Demo recovery
- Failed payment recovery
- No-show recovery
- Cancellation recovery
- Buyer insight recovery
- Checkout or subscription recovery

Each use case should explain the revenue leak, the tool SentientWeb calls, and the recovered business outcome.
